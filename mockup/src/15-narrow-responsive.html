What changes as width drops

The 480 px metadata panel becomes a sheet below the image; nothing is removed, only reordered. Caption first, at‑a‑glance tiles second, grouped fields collapsed.

Caption variants do the work

The tablet renders the full description; the phone renders the short variant with a “full description” disclosure, so no sentence is ever truncated mid‑word.

Tiles reflow 3→2

At‑a‑glance drops to two columns and shows the four facts users open a record for: sol, local time, site/drive and instrument.

Actions pin to the bottom

Download stays reachable with one thumb; secondary actions collapse to icons under 400 px.

Header and rail unchanged

The existing Atlas topbar and 40 px rail stay exactly as they are at every width.
